Why it works

A revenue line with an off switch

01
From our commission

It pays out of our cut, not yours

The royalty is a share of what Boadman earns, our own revenue line. It does not come out of the prize pool, the entry fees, or anything a player pays. You are not taxing your own community.

02
No integration

Zero cost, zero work

No fee to list. No revenue share to us. No minimum, no SDK, no build. Register the title and it is live in the catalogue.

03
You hold it

One switch, and new competitions stop

Two independent kill switches per title, one for tournaments and one for 1v1, and you hold them. Flip one and no new competition starts on that title. Anything already running finishes.

04
First come

One publisher per title, permanently

Registering a title makes your account its publisher of record. One owner, permanent. A genuine reason to move early.

The switch

You are one flip from stopping new play

Illustration of the mechanic

1v1 challengesNew challenges allowed

One switch. New competitions stop.

The switches are yours, per title, held by you. This is the direct answer to the brand-safety worry: you never have to find out from Twitter.

Be clear on the boundary: flipping a switch stops new competitions. Anything already running finishes on its own. That is honest, and still enough to keep control.
