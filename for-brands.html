Why it works

Four things most channels can't say

01
0% commission

Sponsor, and we take nothing

When you sponsor a competition, 100% of your contribution goes into the prize pool. Every coin you put in is a coin a player can win.

02
Auditable ledger

Every coin is a line item

You get a complete, typed, referenced record: prize escrowed, commission, creation fee, releases, refunds. Not soft metrics, a financial record you could hand to finance.

03
Escrowed upfront

Locked before anyone plays

You fund the whole prize before a single player joins, and it sits in escrow. Players can see the pool is real, which is exactly why they enter.

04
Business-verified

No one buys their way in

Every brand passes company-registry, directors and beneficial-owner checks before a coin moves. No fast lane, no bypass. Everyone beside you was checked too.
